## Target version parameters

Approve the F-01–F-11 templates, the working calendar, hosting and retention, the test load and the access roles. Keep manual authorised appointment; candidate recommendations do not make the decision. The technical administrator does not get content permissions automatically. Three languages and internal notifications are part of the target MVP; external integrations are conditional.
