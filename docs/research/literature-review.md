## Synthesis and research gap

[1]–[3] link mentoring to goals, relationships and an organised cycle. That is why the platform separates the goal of the request, the pair consent, the plan and the final evaluation. The number of meetings shows participation, while achieving the outcome is recorded separately. [4]–[6] show that digital availability depends on context and support: a single platform should support exchange and regularity while keeping the mentor's help.

[7]–[10] require treating observation as a cycle of preparation, facts, discussion and action. The AITU map defines the local fields; the studies justify qualitative comments, an agreed focus and a safe environment. That is why the application stores facts and timestamps, while management receives aggregated indicators. [11]–[12] give different grounds for analytics: it can help make sense of feedback but does not replace human interpretation. The effect of visual analytics from a single school case is not claimed as a result achieved by this project.

The project's gap: it is unknown how much more convenient digital support of the specific AITU process is compared with manual paperwork and status tracking. Check: the same scenarios in the prototype and in Word forms, time, errors, completion without help and user comments. Improved student performance or teaching quality is not inferred from how fast forms are filled in. [Evaluation plan](../verification.md).

## Limits of the evidence

The corpus contains 12 scientific publications from 2021–2026; the translations of the Development Visit guidelines are not counted as independent studies. Systematic reviews may include overlapping primary studies, so their results are not added up as independent effects. The contexts of student, medical and school mentoring differ from AITU. The review and conclusions are also presented in the full project presentation.
