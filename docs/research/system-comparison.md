# Comparison of Mentoring Systems

The comparison as of 10 October 2026 is based on the vendors' own pages. "Not confirmed" means the reviewed sources did not confirm the feature, not that the feature is missing. Completion of the comparative analysis is confirmed by the project author; DIP-5 — Done.

Sources: [Together](https://www.togetherplatform.com/page/mentorship-software-platform), [Together Matching Report](https://help.togetherplatform.com/en-us/articles/15877912-matching-report), [MentorcliQ](https://www.mentorcliq.com/), [MentorcliQ reports](https://www.mentorcliq.com/mentoring-software/measure-success-roi), [Chronus](https://chronus.com/software/mentoring-software), [Chronus matching](https://chronus.com/software/mentoring-software/mentor-matching).

| Criterion | Together | MentorcliQ | Chronus | Target AITU platform |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Profiles and matching | By goals and skills | Matching stated | Profiles and MatchIQ | Skills plus request, choice and School decision |
| Self-selection | Participant choice stated | Not confirmed | Self matching | A preferred candidate is optional |
| Guidance | Materials and agendas | Programme guidance stated | Guided mentoring | Joint individual plan |
| Analytics | Participation, goals, feedback | Effectiveness reports | Reports and dashboards | Minimal aggregated information |
| Integrations | HRIS, LMS, calendar | HRIS and reporting systems | Calendar, SSO, HR | Later stage after approval |
| AITU limit of 2 with an exception | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | REG rule with the director's decision |
| Vice-rector's order | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | A separate mandatory step |
| AITU Development Visit | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | Three stages and the map from DV |
| Plan within 10 working days | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | University calendar |

All three products cover the general mentoring category. The project's conclusion: the local document flow and observations require checking the configuration options or a separate implementation. The fit of these products with the AITU process requires a configuration demonstration. A local implementation gives control over the appointment and access rules.
